01 / FRAME THE CHALLENGEBRIEF-PROVIDED

One shipment at a time
doesn’t scale.

The assessment frames booking as a repetitive, error-prone task with rising cognitive load as shipment volume grows. The design opportunity is to shift the unit of work from an individual AWB to a batch that remains inspectable.

THE PROBLEM AS STATED
“Users manually create and manage multiple AWB shipments one at a time.”
Source: supplied assessment brief
01
Repetition compounds

Repeated shipment details are re-entered instead of reused.

02
Errors hide in the handoffs

More manual fields create more opportunities for omissions and inconsistencies.

03
Scale adds cognitive load

Operators must track progress and exceptions across separate bookings.

DESIGN HYPOTHESIS

If operators can create, compare, edit, and validate a batch in one workspace, repetitive effort and avoidable rework may decrease. Both claims need baseline research and usability testing.

IF DETAILS ARE WRONG

Return to the relevant field or AWB. Preserve accepted data and make the change visible.

IF AI IS UNCERTAIN

Ask for confirmation or leave the field unresolved; never silently promote a guess into a booking fact.

Keep submission disabled and link each issue to an actionable fix.

10 / PLACE AI WITH CARECONCEPT STRATEGY

Suggest. Explain.
Wait for approval.

AI is proposed for bounded assistance. The concept does not assume a model, integration, or accuracy level; those require technical and operational validation.

ASSISTANCE POINTAI MAY PROPOSEOPERATOR CONTROL
Request intake

Extract candidate fields from typed instructions or provided documents.

Review source and correct each field before generation.

Repeated data

Suggest reuse from an approved template or sibling AWB.

See what was copied, change its scope, or opt out.

Flight selection

Rank eligible options against entered constraints, if data is available.

Inspect rationale and choose a different option.

Validation

Flag missing, inconsistent, or uncertain values.

Resolve, dismiss with reason, or escalate; never silently submit.

NON-NEGOTIABLE GUARDRAILS

Show uncertainty · preserve source context · log edits and approvals · require human approval for booking submission · fail visibly when data is unavailable.

11 / DEFINE WHAT IS REALPROTOTYPE BOUNDARY

Test the interaction,
not the integration.

The click-through is designed to evaluate comprehension and control across the batch workflow. It is deliberately not a technical proof of AI or system connectivity.

IN SCOPE
  • Six screen-to-screen transitions
  • Illustrative batch, AWB, and validation states
  • Selecting rows and applying a bulk edit
  • Resolving sample validation blockers
  • Reviewing a simulated submission outcome
  • Responsive, keyboard-accessible navigation
OUT OF SCOPE
  • Live CargoWise APIs or authentication
  • Real AWB creation or airline inventory
  • Document upload, OCR, or language model calls
  • Operational compliance or security assurance
  • Measured time savings or accuracy claims
  • Production persistence or audit logging

13 / TELL THE STORYINTERVIEW NARRATIVE

From repetitive work
to a reviewable batch.

A concise presentation arc for walking an interviewer through the reasoning, the prototype, and what still needs to be proven.

01

Set the context. The assessment describes operators creating multiple AWBs one at a time.

02

Name the opportunity. Treat a related group of bookings as one working batch, without hiding shipment-level differences.

03

Show the strategy. Reuse repeated details, make exceptions visible, and keep AI suggestions reviewable.

04

Walk the workflow. Generate drafts, inspect the batch, bulk edit, resolve validation, and review the outcome.

05

Explain the boundary. This prototype demonstrates interaction logic with synthetic data; it does not connect to CargoWise or use a live AI model.

06

Close with the next proof. Observe real booking work, benchmark alternatives, and test the concept against a measured baseline.

THE DESIGN INTENT

Make the batch easier to move through, while making every individual booking easier to trust.
